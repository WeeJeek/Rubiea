EMPYRA — brand assets
=====================
"Empyra" — from the Greek émpyros (ἔμπυρος), "within the fire".

THE MARK
  A flame curling up around a stone, and the stone's own point breaking
  free through the opening at the top.
  The curve belongs to the fire; every straight line belongs to the stone:
    - the flame is an arc weighted 5.6 at the base, tapering to 0.5 at each
      terminal, so it rises and the mark has an unambiguous "up";
    - the stone is cut flat on every edge — a straight-sided kite,
      apex y4, girdle y42, point y90, girdle 37 wide.
  Clearance: 8.4 at the tip, 18.4 at the sides — 2.2:1.

svg/   Production vectors. Wordmarks converted to outlines — your printer
       and your developer need no fonts installed.
png/   Raster exports.

THE OPTICAL SIZE FAMILY — use the right one, this matters
  empyra-symbol.svg           64 px / 22 mm and above   arc 5.6 -> 0.5
  empyra-symbol-compact.svg   40-63 px / 14-22 mm       arc 6.6 -> 1.6
  empyra-symbol-micro.svg     39 px / 14 mm and below   stone only, no arc
Do not scale the standard symbol below 40 px — its fine terminals break.
Switch files instead.

THE GIRDLE SEAM
  In one-colour builds the cut across the stone survives as a 0.25-unit
  hairline — there on inspection, invisible at a glance, never a gap. Foil,
  deboss and engraving close a seam
  that fine, so artwork for those processes widens it to 1.4. Use the files
  in the collateral pack marked "foil" or "deboss" for anything physical.

COLOUR
  Ruby        #7C1226   the flame, the pavilion
  Ember       #C4442C   the crown, accents, links
  Gold Ink    #8F6A22   rules and taglines on screen and in flat print
  Gold Leaf   #C79A4B   metallic reproduction only — foil, gilt, hot stamp
  Bone        #F7F2E9   the ground
  Ink         #241E1C   all body text
Gold Leaf on Bone is a 2.3:1 contrast ratio. Beautiful on foil, illegible in
ink. Use Gold Ink (4.4:1) anywhere the gold is not actually metal.

TYPE
  Cormorant Garamond Light   wordmark, 0.18 em tracking
                             kerning: PY -0.030 em, YR -0.020 em
  Cormorant Garamond Regular any wordmark under 24 px cap height, and every
                             physical process — Light's hairlines drop out
  Jost Light / Regular       taglines and body, 0.30 em tracking on caps
Both SIL Open Font License, free for commercial use. fonts.google.com

CLEAR SPACE
  Empty space equal to half the symbol's height on every side.

NEVER
  Gradients, bevels, drop shadows, outlines. Recolouring outside the palette.
  Evening out the arc's taper or closing the gap — that hands back the
  generic circle this was built to avoid. Stretching or rotating. Setting the
  wordmark in another face. The mark on a photograph without a solid panel.
